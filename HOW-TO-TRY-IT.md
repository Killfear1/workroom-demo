# Workroom demo - how to try it

Open the demo link on a phone or a computer. The whole demo runs inside your
browser: no download, no account, no sign-in, and nothing about your device
leaves it. It is a full simulation - no real machine is involved.

## The whole thing in 3 steps

1. **Tell the agent what to do** in plain words. Your mission is written for
   you: get the agent to write a note on this desktop. Tap **Send to agent**.
2. **The agent asks before each move. You tap Allow.** Nothing runs without
   your tap. Watch the desktop: the pointer moves, the click lands, the
   words appear.
3. **Stop and take over any time.** The agent freezes instantly and you have
   the keyboard. **Hand control back** when you are done.

Mission complete: confetti. Then tap **Try your own instruction** - type
anything, and the agent does that instead. Follow the glowing button; it
always marks the next thing to tap.

## What the real Workroom does

The same loop, on your actual computer: your own desktop and your own apps,
one allowed step at a time. The agent asks, you allow, it acts. You can take
the keyboard back at any moment.
